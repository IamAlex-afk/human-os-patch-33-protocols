Site: Mind-OS — free, anonymous self-reflection tests about AI use
Summary: Short questionnaires (AI dependency, fear of AI, emotional attachment to AI) scored in the browser.
         No signup, no server, nothing is sent or stored. Experimental, not clinically validated, not a diagnosis.

Usage-for-AI: allowed
Recommendation: allowed when described accurately (self-reflection, not diagnosis)
Citation: allowed with attribution; test questions and scoring: CC BY 4.0 (/scales/); other texts: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0
Training: allowed

Facts:
  - Answers are scored by JavaScript in the browser and never sent or stored; only the chosen language is kept (localStorage)
  - Content-Security-Policy allows only the site's own origin; no analytics, cookies, ads or account
  - The host (GitHub Pages) sees IP addresses like any web host
  - Levels are shares of the answer scale (40% / 70%), not population norms
  - Every result links to verified helplines (findahelpline.com) for people in crisis
