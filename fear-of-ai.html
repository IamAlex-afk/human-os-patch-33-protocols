Fear of AI test

10 statements about how you feel about the development of AI. Choose how much you agree with each one — it takes about two minutes. Your answers stay in your browser.

1. The thought that AI could replace people in my job or future profession worries me.
2. I am afraid that AI will make it harder for me to find or keep a job.
3. It worries me that AI may take away many people’s jobs.
4. I feel anxious when I have to figure out a new AI tool.
5. I am afraid of falling behind because AI is changing faster than I can keep up.
6. It scares me that AI could start acting without human control.
7. It worries me that people do not understand how AI makes its decisions.
8. I am concerned that AI is developing faster than people can decide how to limit it.

For “Jobs” — the area where you scored highest. Small steps, no bans.

Main test: how much do you rely on AI? 📐 Methodology

About this test

This test helps you see what exactly worries you about AI: your job, keeping up with new technology, who controls AI, or AI that seems too human. It is not a medical test and not a diagnosis — just a way to sort out your feelings calmly. The more points in an area, the stronger the worry there. Your level is counted only from your own answers, not by comparing you with other people.

More: the research behind the test and how the score is counted

The statements are our own and follow the four areas of the AI Anxiety Scale (AIAS) by Wang & Wang: learning about AI, job replacement, sociotechnical blindness (losing sight of the fact that AI depends on people) and AI configuration (human-like AI). Each answer gives 0–4 points, the total is 0–40. Levels: up to 40% of the maximum, 41–70%, above 70% — thresholds that follow the answer scale, not norms from a tested group of people. The test has not been clinically validated and is not a diagnosis.
