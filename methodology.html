How the Mind-OS test works

What it measures

24 questions in three areas, 8 in each: “Thinking & Memory”, “Anxiety & Emotional Attachment” and “Digital Burnout & Procrastination”. Four more questions measure fear of AI separately.

How the score is calculated

Each answer is worth 0 to 4 points on a five-point scale. In each area, three statements are worded the other way round and scored in reverse. An area gives 0–32 points, the three together 0–96. The fear questions give 0–16.

What the levels mean

Your profile depends on the share of the maximum: up to 40% — “Digital Zen Master”, 41–70% — “Balanced Navigator”, above 70% — “AI-Dependent Delegate”. Fear of AI: 0–4 points — “Low Fear”, 5–8 — “Moderate Fear”, 9–12 — “High Fear”, 13–16 — “Very High Fear”. The map of dependency and fear splits each scale at 50%. These thresholds follow the answer scale itself, not norms from a tested group of people, so your score is not a comparison with others.

Answer check

If you give the same answer to every question in at least two of the three areas, the result shows a note suggesting you retake the test and answer each item on its own.

Research it draws on

The questions are our own. They draw on LLM-D12, a 12-item scale of dependence on large language models tested with 526 participants in the UK (Yankouskaya et al., 2025); the AI Anxiety Scale, AIAS (Wang & Wang); and AIRD, a proposed clinical construct, not an official diagnosis (McNamara & Thornton, Cureus). Mind-OS is not one of these validated scales. Answers on the site also cite Risko & Gilbert, 2016, Gerlich, 2025, Lee et al., 2025 and Kosmyna et al., 2025 (a preprint).

Limits

A self-check shows how you see your own habits; it is not an objective measurement. The test has not been clinically validated, and its result is not a diagnosis. If your use of AI worries you or gets in the way of daily life, talk to a qualified professional.

Your answers stay with you

The score is calculated in your browser, and your answers are stored only on your device (Privacy Policy).
