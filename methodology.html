How the Mind-OS test works

What the tests measure

Mind-OS has open tests of 10 questions each. The AI dependency test on the home page covers three areas: “Pull towards AI”, “Control and consequences” and “Without AI and mood”. The Fear of AI test covers four: “Jobs”, “Keeping up with AI”, “Control over AI” and “Human-like AI”.

How the score is calculated

Each answer gives 0 to 4 points, from left to right on the answer scale, so a test gives 0–40 points. The dependency test asks how often something happens; the fear test asks how much you agree, because fear is an attitude rather than a behaviour. There are no reverse-worded questions: in a study of 374 people, mixing positive and reversed items lowered a test’s reliability (Suárez-Alvarez et al., 2018).

What the levels mean

Up to 40% of the maximum is a low level, 41–70% moderate, above 70% high. The bars show the same share for each area. These thresholds follow the answer scale itself, not norms from a tested group of people, so your score is not a comparison with others.

Answer check

If you give the same answer to every question, the result shows a note suggesting you read each question and answer it on its own.

Research the tests follow

The questions are our own. The dependency test follows areas shared by published scales: the Generative AI Dependency Scale (cognitive preoccupation, negative consequences, withdrawal; Goh, Hartanto & Majeed, 2025), the Conversational AI Dependence Scale (uncontrollability, withdrawal, mood modification, negative impact; Chen et al., 2025) and the Problematic ChatGPT Use Scale (doi:10.1007/s11469-025-01509-y). The fear test follows the four areas of the AI Anxiety Scale (Wang & Wang). Mind-OS is not one of these validated scales. Answers on the site also cite Risko & Gilbert, 2016, Gerlich, 2025, Lee et al., 2025 and Kosmyna et al., 2025 (a preprint).

Limits

A self-check shows how you see your own habits; it is not an objective measurement. The tests have not been clinically validated, and their results are not a diagnosis. If your use of AI worries you or gets in the way of daily life, talk to a qualified professional.

Open questions and scoring

The questions in all 12 languages, the answer options and the scoring rules are published under CC BY 4.0 (scales/), so anyone can check, reuse, translate or test them.

Your answers stay with you

The score is calculated in your browser; your answers are not sent anywhere or stored (Privacy Policy).
