About Mind-OS

What Mind-OS is

Mind-OS is a free, anonymous self-check of how much you rely on AI tools such as ChatGPT. It has 28 questions, runs entirely in your browser, needs no registration and is available in 12 languages. The source code is open on GitHub.

Who makes it

Mind-OS is an independent project by Aleksei Sergeevich Bitkin (ORCID). It is not a medical service and is not affiliated with any AI company. The author’s related work is published on Zenodo.

What it is not

It is not a diagnosis, and the test has not been clinically validated. The result is meant to help you reflect on your habits. If your use of AI worries you or gets in the way of daily life, talk to a qualified professional.

How we work

The questions are our own and draw on published research; factual claims on the site link to their sources (see Methodology). We collect no personal data (Privacy Policy). There are no third-party ads; the site links to the author’s book and to a Ko-fi page where you can support the project. AI tools help with code and translations; facts and sources are checked against the original publications.
