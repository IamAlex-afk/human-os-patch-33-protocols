# Mind-OS Open Scales

Short self-check scales about how people relate to AI, published with their questions, scoring and translations so that
anyone can read, reuse, translate or test them. The live tests: https://iamalex-afk.github.io/human-os-patch-33-protocols/

| File | Scale | Items | Version |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fear-of-ai.json` | Fear of AI test | 10 | 1.0.0 |

## How a scale is scored
Every answer gives 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4 points (left to right on the answer scale); there are no reverse-keyed items.
The total runs from 0 to 4 × items. Level 1: up to 40% of the maximum; level 2: up to 70%; level 3: above 70%.
These thresholds follow the answer scale itself. They are **not** norms measured on a group of people.

## Limits
These scales have **not** been validated in a scientific study and are not diagnostic tools. The questions are our own
wording; each scale names the published research it follows. Mind-OS collects no answers, so we cannot validate them
ourselves — researchers are welcome to, and to report results or corrections via GitHub Issues.
