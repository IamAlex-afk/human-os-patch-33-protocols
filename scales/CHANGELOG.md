# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-20
- ai-dependency: first version, 10 items (craving, control and consequences, withdrawal and mood), frequency scale; follows the Generative AI Dependency Scale (Goh et al. 2025), the Conversational AI Dependence Scale (Chen et al. 2025) and the Problematic ChatGPT Use Scale. Replaces the earlier 28-question test on the home page.
- emotional-attachment-to-ai: first version, 10 items (AI as comfort, AI instead of people, bond with the chatbot), frequency scale; follows Fang, Liu et al. 2025 (arXiv:2503.17473) and the mood-modification area of CAIDS.
- fear-of-ai: first version, 10 items, four areas following the AI Anxiety Scale (Wang & Wang), agreement scale.
- ai-and-thinking (2026-10-21): first version, 10 items (memory, solving problems yourself, checking what AI says), frequency scale; follows research on cognitive offloading (Risko & Gilbert 2016), Gerlich 2025 and Lee et al. 2025.
- ai-and-productivity (2026-10-21): first version, 10 items (time spent on AI, flow of work, unclear benefit), frequency scale; follows Noy & Zhang 2023, Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond 2025, Dell'Acqua et al. 2023 and METR 2025.
